## Known open defect surfaced by independent verification (2026-09-04)
- **TR-001 (Major) — Reddit pacing is not concurrency-safe.** `src/lib/news/reddit.server.ts` (~lines 45-49): the shared pacer computes a delay from `lastRequestAt`, awaits it, then updates the timestamp — it does not reserve a slot or serialize callers, so two waiters wake together and fire in parallel (reproduced: offsets [0, 8023, 8023] ms, 2 concurrent). Violates the "≥8s between every request, never parallel" rule and invites 429s (a live thread fetch returned HTTP 429 in the verification). **Fix:** a single shared queue/mutex that serializes the actual request path and reserves the slot before awaiting; add a 3-concurrent-caller regression asserting strictly ≥8000 ms spacing and max 1 in flight. Also: non-thread URLs fall back to old.reddit.com, and `redd.it` short links are not resolved by that fallback.
