Turn 2 · Interactive desk

Click through the whole desk

Every side-menu item works. Try: + Add a lead or + New story (three ways in: AI from material, write it myself, paste a finished story); Start story on a lead; Run scan now; Sources → + Add a source (one link, paste a list, upload CSV/OPML, or ask AI to find sources); Dark Desk → + Start a file; Follow-ups → + New AI follow-up; Models → change who does each job and at what effort; open a draft to edit the headline and text directly, Redraft…, + Add to story or Check draft against evidence. Anything that takes time shows up under Running now and in the side menu with its stage, current step, elapsed time and last activity. If a job goes quiet for a minute, it says so and offers to retry.

Also wired: Hold and Kill ask for a reason; More ▾ on a lead (edit, merge, send to Dark Desk, follow-up) and on a published story (edit, correction, update, unpublish, legal removal); Suggest 3 headlines; Compare versions; Add a model connection. Stats shows live readers and the pages they are on, where visits come from, reading time, read-through, hour-of-day patterns and trust signals, all counted in aggregate with no per-person tracking.

Screen 2 · Story workbench

One story, left to right: evidence, then words, then publish

The left panel has three tabs (Checks, Sources, Reporting); click them in the preview. The right side is the writing surface: writer status, your headline (protected from redrafts), summary, story, and one row of actions. Screen 1 · Command Center

The desk as a daily run: pick, draft, check, publish

Same features and data as today’s desk, reorganized around the hour or two you spend making the paper. Every action is a real button: yellow for the next step, outlined for secondary actions, red-outlined for Kill. A four-step strip at the top shows where today’s paper stands. "In progress" shows each draft with its one next action. Leads are listed below with Start story, Hold and Kill. The right rail holds Follow-ups, Dark Desk and scan/source status. Lead text is sample data.

Five ideas added: (1) Keyboard triage, working in the preview: click a lead, then J/K to move, H to hold, X to kill, U to undo. (2) An evidence meter on each lead: filled squares are records opened, red outlines are records that could not be opened. (3) A "Tonight’s edition" checklist: a story prints only when every check is done. (4) A phone triage view. (5) The wire keeps "Checked · no change" (green), "Changed" (yellow) and "Could not check" (red dashed, with the reason) visually distinct.
