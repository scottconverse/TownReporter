## What changed

### Lead scores

- **Lead scores come in four parts (method B).** The scan and **Research and score it** rate each lead 1 to 5 on four parts, each with one short reason:

  | Part | The question |
  | --- | --- |
  | Immediacy | Why now? What changed? |
  | Impact | Who gets hit? How many people? How much money? |
  | Conflict | Is there a fight? Opposing sides? |
  | Novelty | Is this new, or routine recurrence? |

  The desk adds the four parts into the lead's newsworthiness (4 to 20). The Queue sort, the warm (10) and hot (14) colors and the priority floors work as before. Migration `0164_lead_score_parts.sql` adds the nullable column `leads.score_parts` and fills nothing: leads filed before this release keep their single score and show no parts. A model reply without valid parts falls back to a plain 0-20 number (#393).

- **Why.** On a 20-lead test with the scan model, 3 runs per lead, one 0-20 number moved 1.6 points between runs on average and kept the same band on 14 of 20 leads. The four parts moved 0.75 points and kept the band on 19 of 20, and they say why (#393).
- **The Queue tooltip shows the parts.** Hover a score: **Newsworthiness N/20**, then for example **Immediacy 4 · Impact 3 · Conflict 1 · Novelty 2** and the reasons (#393).
- **Research and score it uses the 0-20 scale.** It used to ask for 0-100, so a hand-filed lead could show **56**. It now uses the Queue's 0-20 scale and clamps to it (#393).
