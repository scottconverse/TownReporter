This release is **the front page, the designer's first round** — twelve
requested changes to the public front page (units BX, BX2, BX3), merged onto
everything 0.6.77 shipped. Nothing on the desk changed, and no model was
called or loaded to build or check any of it.

## "This week" reads dates the way sources and stories actually write them (units BX, BX2)

The live front page printed an empty **This week** panel while the stories
underneath it named specific dates — a hearing, an event, a second reading, a
deadline — in their own headlines and evidence appendixes. Two separate gaps
caused it.

**The reader was too strict about the shape of a written date.** The panel
reads one structured field per recorded source, `document_date` — the day the
record itself bears — but the reader accepted only a value that started with
an ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. `structuredDate` (`src/lib/story-dates.ts`) now also reads
the forms a captured agenda, notice or filing actually carries: `October 1,
2026`, `Oct. 1, 2026`, `Thursday, October 1st, 2026`, `1 October 2026`, and
`10/1/2026` (numeric dates read month-first, the way this paper writes them,
unless the first number cannot be a month). A value naming no day ("early
September", "last week") is still skipped rather than guessed, and a day with
no year is resolved against the front page's own window and refused if that
window gives no reference day; a day that does not exist is refused by a round
trip through the calendar rather than rolled forward.

**On the live paper, `document_date` came across empty for every one of the
twelve newest published stories** — the dates were not in the rows at all, only
in the stories' own sentences ("… Applications Close **Sept. 29**", "…sends the
plan to an **Oct. 6** public hearing and second reading…"). The sweep now has a
second source: it reads a whole-month-name day out of a story's **headline and
dek only**, never the body, because a headline and dek are the desk's own
one-date summary while a body sentence mentioning a date in passing ("the
council last met on Aug. 12") is indistinguishable from one announcing an
event. The month names are word-bounded and refuse a digit after them, so
"Section 8", "Prop 123" and "3C and 3D" read as no date; a day with no year
takes the year of `published_at` unless that would land more than roughly two
months in the past, in which case it rolls to the coming year; a story naming
the same day twice gets one line; and every line links to the story that named
it. The same source now feeds the article page's own "Dates in this story"
panel, without a link there since the reader is already on that story.

**Filling the panel exposed a second reading defect (unit BX3).** On a
measured copy of the live paper, four of ten printed rows were wrong: two were
the *tail* of a date expression the reader had stopped short of ("Oct. 1-2 …",
"Oct. 1 and 8 …" printed as "-2 instrument collection drive" and "8 regular
meeting"), and two were *places*, not events ("Longmont Senior Center", "Clark
Centennial Park"). The reader now consumes a whole date expression before
cutting the line: a range ("Oct. 1-2") names its first day and discards the
tail, a list ("Oct. 1 and 8") gives each named day inside the window its own
row, and a line that is only a name is refused in favor of the story's own
headline — a headline that is a real clause about what happens is kept even
when it is all-capital title case, but a line a sentence-cut left as a bare
subject is not.

## The lead takes the full row when "This week" is absent

With the panel gone, the lead story's **box** already spanned the full row but
its **text** stopped at roughly half width with a large blank area beside it.
The sole lead now reads as two columns at 901px and wider — section tag and
headline on the left, dek and byline line on the right — while the phone
layout and the row's own grid are unchanged.

## "Around the region" and Opinion hide the same way

When no ground outside the home town has a printed story, that band is not
rendered and the Opinion block takes the full width, with no sentence standing
in for the missing half; both halves still share one row when both are
present. The Opinion block itself is drawn only when the paper has a printed
opinion piece.

## Readers never see the word "misc"

A story filed in the catch-all section used to print its section tag —
"misc" — on the front page grid, in **Latest stories**, on the article page's
breadcrumb, and on section lists. It now prints no tag anywhere on the
reader's side; the desk's own labels (Queue, section picker, editor's
sections) are untouched, and `misc` is still a real section an editor can file
into.

## Small corrections on the page

- A grid cell said "1 read" / "3 read"; it now says "1 min read", matching the
  lead.
- The Opinion block printed "OPINION: <headline>" inside a block already
  titled Opinion; it now prints the headline alone. Display only — the stored
  headline is unchanged.
- **Latest stories** shows six stories plus an "All stories →" link to the
  existing all-stories page; "Load more stories" is gone from the front page,
  and the archive page is unaffected.
- Each latest-stories row printed its date twice; it now prints once, in the
  meta line, which also carries the reading time.
- The county pill said "County" even on a paper whose owner had configured
  one, because `useAreaLabels` never passed the configured county through. A
  configured county now prints as configured; an unset one still prints
  "County".
- Three strips came off the front page ("Find your way around.", "A little
  easier on the eyes.", "Your community. An open record."); text size stays in
  the top bar and About stays in the footer.
- **"Useful around town"** was removed and its two links — City Council Votes
  and Utility Bill Analyzer — moved into the footer; no link was dropped.
- The trust strip ("Reporting you can trace to the record / Corrections in the
  open / How we report") is unchanged.

## The phone

At 390px the top bar fits in two rows instead of three, with the action
buttons keeping their 44px targets and their labels moving to icons. The
geography pills are one row that scrolls sideways instead of wrapping, in the
same order, with the scrollbar hidden.

## A CI fixture stopped reading this machine's own state

`scripts/ci-stage-start.ps1` scenario 4 read the real machine's
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\TownReporter\staged-copy.json` — this machine's own record of
where a staged copy lives — so it passed or failed depending on whether this
machine had ever staged one, and would fail on any machine that had. The
fixture now points `LOCALAPPDATA` at its own temp folder for the run and
restores the real value in `finally`, the way `ops-scripts.test.mjs` already
requires of another fixture.

## What a reader sees

This release **is** reader-facing. On the public front page: "This week" now
fills in with dates read from source records and, failing that, from a
story's own headline and dek; "Around the region" and the Opinion block each
disappear cleanly instead of leaving a blank half when there's nothing to
show; the lead story's headline and dek now use the full row when "This
week" is absent; no story anywhere prints the internal label "misc"; reading
time reads "1 min read" instead of "1 read"; the Opinion block doesn't repeat
the word "Opinion"; **Latest stories** ends in an "All stories →" link
instead of "Load more stories" and shows each story's date once; a configured
county prints correctly; three promotional strips and the "Useful around
town" panel are gone from the front page (their links live in the footer
now); and the phone top bar is more compact.

## Migrations

**None.** `git diff origin/deepseek/redesign-mount-a..HEAD --stat -- migrations`
shows no output — nothing under `migrations/` changed since the 0.6.77 merge
base.

## Compatibility

No existing action is removed or changed in what it does on the editor desk.
On the public front page, several elements are removed or relocated (three
promotional strips, "Useful around town", "Load more stories") but every link
they carried is still reachable, from the top bar, the footer, or the
all-stories archive page. Nothing changes shape at the data layer: no
migration, no new column, no new route, no new scheduled task, no new
service, no new port.

## Limits

- The database behind every check for this release is one these checks built
  in memory; the live paper's own `document_date` rows were read from
  `report.ts`'s coercion and from story text quoted in the unit reports, not
  from a dump of the live database, so no claim here was measured against the
  live paper's own rows.
- The design assets named in the underlying brief do not exist under
  `docs/design/redesign-v2-2026-09-26/`; the drawings used are the ones in
  `docs/design/handoff-2026-09-26/`.
- The phone-width rules were measured on the built page at 390px; nothing in
  this change was checked against a real phone.
- The date sweep deliberately does not read a story's body text: a date in a
  headline or dek is the desk's own summary and is nearly always the event's
  own date, while a date inside a body sentence reads identically whether it
  names a past meeting, a quoted document, or an upcoming event, and reading
  it would put events on the front page that no story announced. A story that
  names its one date only in its body contributes nothing to the panel. The
  source also reads month-name days only; "tomorrow", "next Tuesday" and "in
  two weeks" are not read.
- No model was loaded or called to build or check this release.
