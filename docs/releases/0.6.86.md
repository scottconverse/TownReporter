## What changed

### Killed. Undo on the lead's own row

After fast Kill from the row **Kill** button, the **X** key, Today's row **Kill**, or bulk **Kill**, the lead's row stays where it was. It shows one line: **Killed.**, the lead's headline and an **Undo** button.

- The row stays about 10 seconds, then leaves the list.
- **Undo** brings the lead back at once, the same as the bottom strip's Undo.
- Bulk Kill gives each killed row its own **Killed.** line and **Undo**. The bulk strip stays too.
- The bottom strip (**Lead killed.** and **Undo**) is still there. Nothing was removed.
- **Undo** is at least 44 px tall on a phone, with no sideways scroll at 390 px.
- More -> **Kill with a reason** and the story page's **Kill this lead** dialog remain.

### Dark Desk uses the file's picked model for a new round

After **Stop**, the round ends as **Cancelled by the editor** and **Keep investigating** works again. The next round starts on the model shown in **Digging model for this file**.

- On a stopped round, **Retry** uses the model and thinking effort shown in the picker, not the stopped round's model.
- The retry note names the model that was queued, for example **Retry queued on DeepSeek v4.1 Flash 1017.**
- **Retry on next model** is kept.
