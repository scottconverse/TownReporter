## What changed

### The AI finds duplicate leads itself

The desk now makes one extra model call per scan batch. It compares every new lead with recent desk leads from the last 30 days (at most 300, including killed leads), printed stories from the last 30 days (at most 150), and earlier leads of the same scan. The call runs only where the word rule found nothing. If the call fails, the scan files leads as before.

- **Same story, no new fact:** the old lead is marked as **Came back** (resurfaced), and nothing new is filed. A lead killed as a duplicate stays killed.
- **Same story with a new fact:** the new lead is filed as **Held** and linked to the old lead as **Possible duplicate**.
- **Same as an earlier lead of the same scan:** the new lead is merged into it, and the source links are joined.
- **Matches a printed story:** the new lead is filed as **Held**, not Open.
- **Result:** an AI duplicate never lands in Open.

### Fast Kill is a small Undo strip

The fast Kill paths are the row Kill button, the X key and Today row Kill. They no longer show reason chips. They show one line, **Lead killed.**, with **Undo**, for 8 seconds. The strip does not move when the mouse is over it, so the close box is easy to click. On a phone it fits inside the screen with a 16 px gap on each side.

Bulk Kill works from ticked rows and the Kill button. It does not ask **Why?**. It kills at once and shows one strip, **Killed N leads.**, with **Undo**.

### The full Kill paths remain

More menu -> **Kill with a reason** and the story page's **Kill this lead** still open the full **Kill this lead** dialog. The dialog keeps quick fills, a reason in the editor's own words, and a link. **Kill as duplicate** remains in the More menu.

### Operations

`ops/promote.ps1` no longer crashes while writing its verify log (finding G27-PR-1).
