# TownReporter 0.6.46

Release date: 2026-09-13. [GitHub release](https://github.com/scottconverse/TownReporter/releases/tag/v0.6.46) · [Windows installation](../windows-install.md) · [Reader guide](../reader.md) · [Editor desk](../editor-desk.md).

This release implements the approved Astra public newspaper design. It includes the full searchable and paginated published archive, configured sections, browser-local bookmarks, light/dark appearance, three reading sizes, sharing and RSS, real evidence and comparisons, reporting policies and a correction email form. Existing article URLs and editor workflows remain available.

The source walkthrough exposed a trailing-slash mismatch between published citations and canonical saved records; this release repairs that match without exposing unpublished sources. Corrections are restricted to the publication's newsroom.

Correction email is a prepared mailto message: the reader reviews and sends it in their email application. TownReporter uses townreporter@gmail.com. The website does not deliver email or publish reader correspondence automatically. Browser preferences and saved stories do not sync across devices.

Verification includes a built staging copy of the publication, article and evidence navigation, reader controls and responsive/dark-mode checks. The release verification receipt records completed checks and any limits. The tagged source, Windows package and live deployment are distinct artifacts; deployment is confirmed separately by the operator.

Existing installations retain their data and configuration, take a backup, and follow their reviewed upgrade procedure. This design change requires no new database migration. Existing AI-provider and editorial verification limits from [0.6.45](0.6.45.md) remain.
