## What changed

### Assignment-based newsroom reporting

Editors can assign a meeting or lead and receive a structured reporting package beside the draft: a coverage ledger, source-linked claims with locators, a score and readiness tier, explicit unknowns, and a run receipt. The package is private editorial evidence and remains separate from the public story. A fresh check against retained documents is shown separately from the reporter's recorded status, current copy and the editor's judgment.

Dated, sourced corrections, dispositions and source notes are scoped to the relevant lead and assignment for later runs. A targeted follow-up creates a new run and new result version while preserving the earlier draft, notes and checked states. Source inventory and source-selection signals make observations, due work and replacement candidates reviewable; they do not automatically remove or accept sources.

### Meeting reporting (six existing WR1 commits)

The existing candidate commits add whole-meeting coverage with an editor-visible action ledger. They account for agenda items and recorded actions, allow editors to review and set item treatment, group coverage by the packet's agenda, count votes, keep one lead, follow the meeting's own procedure, use plain labels, add a section for a second major item, and follow the packet's names and spellings. The ledger shows evidence and disposition for actions; an unread segment or item without editorial treatment remains visible.

### Documentary checks and correction handling

The reporting package preserves the distinction between what the reporting run recorded and what a current check of retained documents supports. Claim-specific locators and references are shown with that current check. A locator problem, unsupported compound claim, wrong page or wrong subject remains a reason to hold or narrow the claim; the check does not bulk-upgrade statuses. Corrections can be retrieved in their assignment scope without making the editor note independent evidence or allowing it to override contrary documents automatically.
