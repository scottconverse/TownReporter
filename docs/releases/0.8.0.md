## What changed

### Desk (#438)

- **Buttons and disclosures look like controls.** On `/desk/registry` the section headers, the category and language menu and the four freshness cards now draw a 1px edge instead of a hairline rule. On Drafts the **Select all shown** label draws the same edge around its box, with a little padding. A pressable thing now reads as pressable, and each edge meets the desk's contrast floor.
- **The Opinion page notice is a paragraph.** The line that says the paper's editorial voice has not been saved is now a paragraph rather than a generic status region, so its **Paper's editorial voice** link is read as prose. The words did not change.

### Nightly browser tests (#438)

Four nightly browser walks had failed since Nov 22 while the app moved on. They now pass against the app again. Two were stale: the 0.5.1 walk again presses the bulk Kill confirm, and the scan desk walk no longer waits for a batch-size warning that was removed in November. One opened an unknown registry id that answers 404 on purpose, and now opens a published entry. The clickable guard found the plain-looking controls above, and they were fixed rather than the guard.
