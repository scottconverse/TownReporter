## What changed

### Lead scores on every desk screen

- **Today and the Queue** print the four parts under the headline, for example **Immediacy 4 · Impact 3 · Conflict 1 · Novelty 2**. The Queue tooltip still shows the reasons.
- **The story page** shows **Score N/20**, the parts line, and one reason per part. A part with an empty reason is left out.
- A lead filed before 0.6.91 shows only its total. A lead that was never scored shows no score (it used to show **0/20**) (#395).

### Publish all drafts

On **Drafts**, **Publish all drafts (N)** publishes your finished stories in one step:

1. The button counts every draft whose row says **Your draft** or **Ready to check**, from the whole list. Running, failed and "needs you" drafts (a name to review, evidence to check, imported text) are left out. With nothing to publish, the button is hidden.
2. Press it. **Publish these stories?** lists each story with its section.
3. Press **Publish N stories**. Each story prints in the section shown, through the same checks as the story page's Publish.
4. The result lists **Published (N)**, each with a link, and **Not published (N)**, each with the reason. A story refused by a warning has **Publish anyway** for that story only. A hard refusal has no such button.

One request carries at most 25 stories; the desk sends a longer list in batches (#395).
