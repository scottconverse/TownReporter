## City Information

- **City:** Boulder
- **State:** Colorado
- **County:** Boulder County
- **Population:** ~105,000
- **Government type:** Council-Manager (9 at-large council members, elected mayor)
- **Notable:** Boulder uses Ranked Choice Voting for mayoral elections (since 2023).
  Transitioning to even-year elections for all council seats starting 2026.

## Notes

- Boulder uses **Ranked Choice Voting** for mayoral elections (implemented 2023).
  The Boulder County Clerk's Office oversees RCV implementation.
- The **Boulder Valley Comprehensive Plan** is undergoing a major 10-year update
  (BVCP-25-0001). Draft released March 3, 2026. Comment period through April 6.
  Adoption expected summer 2026.
- Boulder has a new **Affordable Housing Impact Fee** (Ordinance 8712, effective
  Jan 31, 2026) on replacement homes and substantial additions.
- The **Access Management and Parking Strategy (AMPS)** project is rolling out
  changes through April 2026.
- Boulder 8 TV and two city podcasts (**Let's Talk Boulder**, **Somos Boulder**)
  are additional official information channels.
