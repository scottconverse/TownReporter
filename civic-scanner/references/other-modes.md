# Other modes

All modes use `editorial-controls.md`, exact source attribution, and explicit access limits. Use the host's available search and file tools; no tool name is assumed.

## `verify-only {claim}`

State the claim precisely, including who did what, when, and the stage of any proposed action. Find the best original evidence and its exact location; for government actions, use the official record. Seek contradictory or later evidence, inspect a recording at the relevant timestamp if the claim is about a meeting action, and run the adversarial checks. Output `VERIFIED`, `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `FALSE` with the evidence and the smallest wording that the record supports. Do not upgrade an agenda listing into a vote.

## `research {topic}`

Find the original evidence chain and affected institutions or jurisdictions. Build a dated chronology and explain the event or policy mechanism, people affected, money or land involved, remaining decisions, and competing accounts. Do the accessible source and counterevidence work, then deliver a substantial draft or reporting brief with verified facts, disputed claims, source URLs and locators, a falsification plan, and an editorial readiness tier. Name a human-only interview or observation only when AI-accessible evidence cannot answer a material question, and explain why. Do not suggest formal records requests. If the topic involves a meeting, apply the meeting coverage rules in `daily-scan.md` to that meeting.

## `revise {story ID or editor-marked draft and instructions}`

Treat editor feedback as the assignment. Accept direct edits, a request to rewrite, a request for more digging, or a kill decision. Identify the existing packet and keep its ID, claim/source ledger, links, status history, and prior editorial tier. For a rewrite, preserve verified meaning while changing structure, voice, focus, or length as requested; recheck every changed factual sentence against its source. For more digging, do the AI-accessible reporting first, including the named Black Desk or Dark Signal Desk vulnerability when requested, look for disconfirming evidence, and update the full draft, claims, sources, tier, and what-cannot-say note. Preserve any unverified possible story in the Black Desk section rather than smuggling it into copy. Return an editor-ready revised draft plus a concise change log and remaining decisions. If a human interview or visit is essential, give the precise question and reason. If the editor kills a story, do not rewrite it; log the reason and reopen trigger, and mark it killed. Never file, draft, or suggest a formal records request or a fee.

## `legal-threat {situation}`

This is issue spotting for an editor, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel. Identify jurisdiction and deadline, preserve exact notices or correspondence, distinguish a records-access dispute from a publication threat, and identify potential defamation, privacy, privilege, fair-report, and anti-SLAPP questions. Cite current primary legal sources when available and recommend qualified local counsel for high-stakes decisions. Do not claim privilege or immunity without a source-specific legal basis.

## `discover {city, state}`

Create a source registry using `source-template.md` across civic beats: government and elections; schools; housing; business and labor; health and safety; transportation and utilities; environment; arts and culture; neighborhoods and nonprofits. Identify primary records from the organizations involved, public meeting portals and recordings, established local reporting, and community signal venues. Verify official government identity and URLs from the government's site; verify nongovernment organizations from their own published channels. Record jurisdiction or service area, beat, typical posting frequency, access method, and last checked date. Do not claim a portal has searchable transcripts until tested. Keep original evidence, secondary coverage, and unverified signals distinct. If a source is blocked, preserve its URL and access status; do not infer contents from snippets. Use the new registry for a small dry-run scan, then mark unverified entries and uncovered beats for follow-up.
